Edita's Story

Why Edita Chose Real Estate

Edita's journey into real estate began long before she became an agent herself. Around 20 years ago, she was selling one of her own rental properties. Like many homeowners, she wanted to choose the best person for the job. She selected an agent who seemed very successful — someone regularly featured in the local paper and known for selling a large number of homes. Edita believed that experience and visibility would mean she was in safe hands and would achieve a strong price.

But the experience was very different from what she expected. The appraisal price initially presented to her helped secure the listing. Then, only a week later, she was told the price needed to be lowered because buyers were supposedly not prepared to pay what the property had been appraised at. Communication became difficult. Calls were not returned, updates were limited, and Edita was left wondering what was actually happening with her sale.

She soon realised that the agent was handling so many listings that there was little time for her property. Much of the work was passed to a very new salesperson who was still learning and did not always know what to do. Instead of feeling supported, informed and confident, Edita felt left in the dark during one of the biggest financial decisions a person can make.

That experience stayed with her. It gave Edita a deep understanding of how vulnerable selling a home can feel when you do not know whether your agent is truly working for you, who has viewed your home, what buyer feedback is coming through, or what the next step should be. She decided to enter real estate to give people the experience she wished she had received: honest advice from the beginning, clear communication throughout, and someone who remains personally involved from the first conversation through to settlement.

Today, Edita never forgets what it feels like to be on the other side of the sale. Her approach is built around being available, keeping vendors fully informed, explaining the process clearly, and making sure no client feels like just another listing. For Edita, selling property is not simply about putting a home on the market — it is about guiding people through an important moment with care, straight answers and a genuine commitment to achieving the best possible result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Edita Andrijasevic

Do you operate across all of West Auckland, or just specific suburbs?

I cover most of the West Auckland region. Whether you are selling a family home in Glen Eden or Glendene, a modern townhouse in New Lynn, or a lifestyle property out in Oratia and Titirangi, I understand the unique buyer demographics and value drivers of each distinct neighbourhood.

How many years of experience do you have in the West Auckland property market?

I bring years of hands-on local real estate experience to your campaign. Having navigated through multiple property cycles — from roaring seller's markets to tight economic shifts — I know exactly how to adjust strategies to ensure your home stands out and sells.

How do I know you will get the absolute highest price for my home?

I am a tough, disciplined negotiator who treats your equity like my own. I don't just act as a message-carrier between parties; I actively protect your reserve price, counter low offers strategically, and drive competition to extract every dollar from the market.

What makes your property marketing strategy different from other agents?

I avoid generic, passive listings. I design hyper-targeted, digital-first campaigns that combine premium videography, social media algorithm targeting, and strategic staging advice to grab the attention of active buyers where they are actually looking.

How do you determine the right listing price in today's market?

I merge real-time suburb sales data with deep market insight. We look at recent local sales, current active listings, and buyer sentiment so we can confidently price your home to attract genuine, competitive offers from day one.

What are your commission rates and marketing fees?

My commission structure is transparent, highly competitive, and reflects the premium service and results I deliver. I offer flexible marketing packages tailored to your property's needs, ensuring every dollar spent goes directly toward maximising your final sale price.

How does your agency network help find active buyers?

When you list with me, you aren't just hiring one agent — you tap into a large, interconnected network. Your property is immediately shared with an extensive internal database of pre-qualified local and out-of-area buyers, and with agents from other agencies if they have buyers specifically looking to move out west.

How long will it take to sell my West Auckland home?

While market conditions dictate general timing, my combination of targeted marketing and accurate pricing is designed to beat the standard local days-on-market average. I will provide you with an honest, data-backed timeline during our consultation.

How often will I receive updates once my property goes live?

You will always be fully informed. I provide comprehensive feedback immediately after every open home, structured weekly marketing reports detailing your digital engagement, and instant communication the moment a serious enquiry or offer comes through.

What is the first step to listing my home with you?

We begin with a casual, obligation-free property consultation. I will visit your home, provide concrete advice on quick ways to add value before selling, and outline a custom sales strategy that matches your timeline and future moving plans.
