Choosing A Method

Asking price, auction or deadline sale?

The method is part of the price. It decides how buyers find your home and how competition between them works.

Asking price

Clear for buyers and easy to search. Works best when there is good sales evidence and a price can be set with confidence.

Auction

No price is advertised and buyers compete in real time. Suits homes with strong demand. See property auctions.

Deadline sale

Written offers by a set date, with conditions allowed. Suits homes where buyers need finance or reports. See deadline sale advice.

How Buyers Search

Why price brackets matter

Most buyers search property websites using price filters. A home priced just above a common bracket can drop out of the searches of the very buyers most likely to want it. Pricing advice looks at where your home will appear, not just the number.

Signs the price is working

  • Steady enquiries and open home numbers in the first two weeks.
  • Second viewings and building report requests.
  • Buyers asking about process and timing, not only price.

Signs it needs review

  • Online views without enquiries.
  • The same price objection from different buyers.
  • Buyers comparing your home with cheaper listings.

Repeated price reductions can train buyers to wait for the next one. A clear buyer feedback review helps you decide early, and why homes do not sell explains the other reasons a home can sit.

The Evidence

Where pricing advice comes from

Recent sales of genuinely similar homes, current competing listings and the latest suburb market reports. If you want the evidence laid out for your own home, start with a free property appraisal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Property Pricing Advice FAQs

How do I decide what price to ask for my house?

Start with recent sales of genuinely similar homes nearby, then consider current competing listings, buyer demand and how buyers search online. Edita shows you this evidence for your home.

Is it better to sell by auction or with a price?

It depends on the home and the market. Auctions suit homes with strong demand. A price or deadline sale can suit homes where buyers need finance or reports.

What happens if my house is priced too high?

Fewer of the right buyers see it, interest can fade and the home can go stale. Price reductions later can make buyers wait for more.

Should I choose the agent with the highest appraisal?

Not on that alone. Ask each agent to show the sales behind their number. A high figure without evidence may only be there to win the listing.

Can Edita review the price if my home is already on the market?

Yes. Edita can review the buyer feedback and sales evidence and give you an honest second opinion.
