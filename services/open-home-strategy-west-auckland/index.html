Before The First Open Home

The first weekend matters most

Buyer interest is usually highest in the first one or two weekends of a campaign. That is when the home needs to look its best and the open home plan needs to be right.

Timing that suits buyers

Weekend open homes for working buyers, with private viewings in between for people who cannot make the set times.

Presentation ready

Lighting, temperature, smell, clutter and outdoor areas. See home presentation advice and whether home staging is worth it.

Information on hand

Floor plans, the LIM, title and building reports where available, so buyers can answer their own questions on the spot.

During And After

Turning visitors into offers

At the open home

  • Every visitor is welcomed and registered.
  • Edita notes what each buyer looks at, asks about and compares the home with.
  • Questions about price, conditions and timing are answered carefully, without weakening your position.

After the open home

  • Interested buyers are followed up while the home is fresh in their mind.
  • Second viewings and building inspections are arranged quickly.
  • You get a clear summary of who came, what they said and who is likely to make an offer.

That summary is the start of Edita’s buyer feedback review. If more than one buyer moves at once, the process becomes multi-offer management.

Your Home, Your Rules

Keeping your home safe during open homes

Put away valuables, jewellery, medication, keys and personal documents before each open home. Pets are best taken out for the hour. If the property is tenanted, open homes are arranged with the tenant’s agreement and proper notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Open Home Strategy FAQs

How many open homes will my property need?

It depends on buyer demand and the selling method. Most interest usually comes in the first two weekends, with private viewings arranged in between.

Should I be at home during the open home?

Usually not. Buyers talk more freely and take their time when the owner is not there.

Do I get feedback after each open home?

Yes. Edita follows up interested visitors and reports back on who came, what they said and who is likely to act.

Can buyers view my home outside open home times?

Yes. Private viewings can be arranged for genuine buyers who cannot attend the set times.

Can a tenanted property have open homes?

Yes, with the tenant’s cooperation and the notice required under tenancy law. Edita plans viewings with the tenant to keep things smooth.
