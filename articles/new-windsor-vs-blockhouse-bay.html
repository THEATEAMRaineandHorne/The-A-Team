New Windsor vs Blockhouse Bay: How Buyers Compare the Two

Many buyers who look at your New Windsor home will also be looking in Blockhouse Bay. Here is how they tend to compare the two, and what that means for your price and your campaign.

By Edita Andrijasevic · Published 26 October 2026 · Updated 26 October 2026

If you are selling in New Windsor, many of the buyers who come through your open home will have viewed a Blockhouse Bay home the same weekend. The two suburbs sit side by side, share a similar mix of family homes, and appeal to many of the same buyers. Buyers who start looking in one often end up in the other.

That comparison shapes how buyers judge your price. This guide looks at how buyers tend to weigh New Windsor against Blockhouse Bay, and what that means for pricing and presenting a New Windsor home. For local selling support, see the New Windsor real estate agent service page.

The quick answer

Buyers usually see Blockhouse Bay for its coastal setting and village, and New Windsor for practical value: family homes, good access to SH20 and the city, and popular school zones on some streets. A New Windsor home sells best when it is priced against genuine New Windsor sales and its practical strengths are made clear, so buyers can see why it makes sense next to the Blockhouse Bay homes they are also viewing.

Why buyers look at both suburbs

New Windsor sits between Blockhouse Bay, Lynfield and Mount Roskill. For many buyers, the suburb name matters less than what they can get for their budget: the right number of bedrooms, a usable section, parking, a reasonable commute and, for some families, a particular school zone. Because New Windsor and Blockhouse Bay offer many of those things, buyers often keep both on their list.

How the two suburbs compare

These are the points buyers most often weigh up. Every street and property is different, so treat them as general patterns rather than rules.

SettingBlockhouse Bay has its beach, waterfront reserves and village shops. New Windsor is quieter and more inland, with local parks and reserves.
AccessNew Windsor is well placed for SH20 and the Waterview Tunnel, which appeals to buyers commuting to the city, the airport or south Auckland.
HousingBoth have family homes, brick-and-tile homes, units and cross-lease properties. The mix differs street by street.
PriceBlockhouse Bay has often been seen as the higher-priced suburb, but the difference changes over time and varies by property.

Price is not a fixed gap

It is easy to assume Blockhouse Bay will always sell for more. Its coastal setting and village do appeal to many buyers. But the difference between the two suburbs is not fixed. It depends on the street, the property type, the title, the land and the condition of the home, and the two suburbs' prices have not always moved in the same direction over the past year. For current figures, see the New Windsor market report and the Blockhouse Bay market report.

School zones run street by street

Some streets in both suburbs are in sought-after school zones, including the Lynfield College zone. For families focused on a particular school, zone status can matter more than which suburb a home is in. Boundaries run street by street and can change, so always check the exact address with the school or the Ministry of Education's school finder before relying on it or putting it in your marketing. The guide on how much Lynfield College zone adds to property values explains how zone status affects buyer interest.

Who chooses New Windsor over Blockhouse Bay

Buyers who end up in New Windsor often share a few priorities:

  • Families comparing school zones who find an in-zone New Windsor home suits their budget or needs better than the options in Blockhouse Bay.
  • Commuters who have tested the drive and found that SH20 and the Waterview Tunnel make New Windsor work well for them.
  • First-home buyers looking for a family suburb in this part of Auckland at a price that fits their lending.
  • Investors looking for steady rental demand from families who want to live in the area.

Buyers who choose Blockhouse Bay instead are often drawn by the beach, the village or a particular outlook. Knowing which group your home suits best helps decide how it should be marketed.

What this means for pricing a New Windsor home

The most reliable evidence for your price is usually recent sales of similar homes in New Windsor itself: on comparable streets, with the same title type, a similar section and similar condition. Blockhouse Bay sales are useful for understanding what buyers are choosing between, but they should not be used on their own to set a New Windsor price.

Pricing a New Windsor home as though it were in Blockhouse Bay can leave it sitting on the market while buyers compare it with homes that seem better value. Pricing it without looking at what buyers can buy in Blockhouse Bay can miss the competition they are weighing it against. Property pricing advice should consider both.

How to present a New Windsor home against Blockhouse Bay listings

Buyers who have just viewed a Blockhouse Bay home will be comparing more than the price. To help your home hold its own:

  • Lead with practical strengths. Confirmed school zone where it applies, SH20 access, usable land, parking and an easy layout are the things New Windsor buyers often value most.
  • Help buyers picture daily life. Show how the outdoor areas, storage and garage space work, since many buyers are comparing how easy each home will be to live in.
  • Be upfront about road noise or busy routes. If your home is near a busy road, choose open home times carefully and present the interior calmly. Buyers notice, and it is better to address it than to hope they do not.
  • Have documents ready. A current LIM, the title and, for cross-lease homes, the flats plan help buyers feel confident, especially when they are weighing up several homes at once.

If you are selling near the boundary

Homes close to the New Windsor and Blockhouse Bay boundary are often compared most directly. For these homes, recent sales on both sides of the boundary may be relevant, and the marketing should make clear what buyers get on your street, rather than relying on the suburb name. Frequently asked questions

Is New Windsor cheaper than Blockhouse Bay?

Blockhouse Bay has often been seen as the higher-priced of the two because of its coastal setting and village, but the difference is not fixed. It varies by street, property type, title and condition, and the two suburbs' prices have not always moved in step. Compare current sales in both suburbs for homes like yours, using the New Windsor and Blockhouse Bay market reports for the latest figures, rather than relying on a general rule.

Why do buyers compare New Windsor with Blockhouse Bay?

The two suburbs sit side by side and share many of the same things buyers want: family homes on good-sized sections, popular school zones on some streets, and access to SH20, New Lynn and the city. Buyers who start looking in one suburb often end up looking in the other once they see what their budget can buy.

Are New Windsor and Blockhouse Bay in the same school zones?

Some streets in both suburbs are in sought-after zones, including Lynfield College, but zone boundaries run street by street and change over time. Always check the exact address with the school or the Ministry of Education's school finder before relying on it or advertising it.

Should I use Blockhouse Bay sales to price my New Windsor home?

Use them with care. The most relevant evidence is usually recent sales of similar homes in New Windsor itself, on comparable streets, with the same title type and in similar condition. Blockhouse Bay sales can help show what buyers are choosing between, but they should not be used on their own to set a New Windsor price.

How can a New Windsor home stand out against Blockhouse Bay listings?

Lead with the strengths buyers value in New Windsor: confirmed school zone where it applies, quick access to SH20 and the Waterview Tunnel, usable land, parking and a home that is easy to live in. Present it well, have documents ready, and price it against genuine comparable sales so buyers can see why it makes sense next to the Blockhouse Bay homes they are also viewing.
