Fee vs result

Why the cheapest fee rarely leaves you with the most money

What matters is what you walk away with: sale price, minus commission, marketing and legal costs. The sale price is by far the biggest number in that sum, so a small difference in price outweighs a big difference in fee.

The Green Bay maths

On a $1,100,000 Green Bay house:

  • An agent who discounts commission by 0.5% saves you about $5,500.
  • An agent who gets just 1% more for the home earns you about $11,000.
  • An agent who gets 2% more earns you about $22,000, roughly Edita's whole commission on that home.

That is why the right question isn't "who is cheapest?" but "who will get the highest price, and how will they do it?"

Red flags when comparing agent quotes

  • An appraisal far above everyone else's. Ask for the comparable sales behind it. Over-appraising to win the listing, then cutting the price later, is the oldest trick in real estate.
  • Fees quoted with and without GST. Convert every quote to a GST-inclusive dollar figure at the same expected price.
  • A low fee with heavy upfront marketing. Add everything you would pay if the home doesn't sell.
  • No plan for updates. Ask exactly how and when you'll hear about open homes, feedback and offers. Edita calls after every open home and sends a written report on the same day each week.

How it works in NZ

How real estate commission is calculated in New Zealand

There is no set rate in New Zealand. Each agency sets its own structure, usually one of two kinds:

  • Tiered percentage: a higher rate on the first part of the price (often the first few hundred thousand dollars) and a lower rate on the rest.
  • Base fee plus a percentage: a fixed amount plus a percentage of the whole price.

Both are almost always quoted plus GST. The government's Settled.govt.nz guide notes that some agents also charge an administration fee, so ask whether one applies.

Your rights before you sign

  • The commission, how it's calculated and the estimated amount at your expected price must be in your written agency agreement.
  • Your agent must give you the Real Estate Authority's guide to the agency agreement.
  • You can cancel a sole agency agreement by written notice up to 5pm on the first working day after you receive a copy.
  • You can check any agent's licence and disciplinary record on the REA public register. Edita is Licensee 20027060, Agent class.
Frequently Asked Questions

Real estate agent fees: common questions

How much does a real estate agent cost in West Auckland?

Most agencies charge a tiered commission: commonly 2.95% to 3.95% on the first $400,000 to $500,000 and around 2% to 2.5% on the rest, plus GST, sometimes with an administration fee on top. The final figure depends on the property and where it is.

What is a standard real estate commission in NZ?

There is no set or standard rate in New Zealand; every agency sets its own. Most use a tiered structure (a higher percentage on the first part of the price and a lower one on the rest) or a base fee plus a percentage, and almost all quote commission excluding GST, so add 15% to compare like with like.

Is real estate commission negotiable?

Commission is always agreed between you and the agent and written into the agency agreement before you sign. Edita's commission is usually already lower than the common tiered structure; the final figure depends on the property and where it is, and is confirmed in writing at your appraisal. A lower fee is only a saving if the agent still gets you the best price.

Does Edita charge an administration fee?

No. Edita's commission is the only agency fee: there is no administration fee or other hidden charge. Commission is quoted plus GST, written into your agency agreement before you sign, and only payable if your home sells. Marketing is a separate cost you choose and approve before the campaign starts.

Who pays the real estate agent's commission?

In New Zealand the seller pays the listing agent's commission, not the buyer. It is normally deducted from the buyer's deposit or the sale proceeds once the sale has gone unconditional or settled, as set out in your agency agreement — you do not pay it out of pocket before the sale.

Do I pay commission if my house doesn't sell?

No commission is payable if your home does not sell under the agency agreement. Marketing is different: it is a separate cost you approve before the campaign starts, so ask any agent in writing what is payable if the home does not sell.

Is GST added to real estate commission?

Yes. Commission in New Zealand is almost always quoted plus GST at 15%, including Edita's rates. On a $1,100,000 Green Bay sale, $22,000 + GST becomes $25,300 in total. Always compare agents on the GST-inclusive dollar figure at your expected price, and ask whether an administration fee is added on top.

How much are lawyer fees to sell a house in NZ?

Conveyancing for a straightforward sale is commonly around $1,500 to $2,500 including disbursements, but fees vary between firms, so get a written quote from your own property lawyer before you list.

Is the cheapest real estate agent the best choice?

Not usually. On a $1,100,000 home, getting just 1% more for it is worth $11,000 — more than a typical commission discount. Compare agents on the net amount you are likely to walk away with, using their evidence of recent sale prices, not on the fee alone.

Can I sell my house privately to avoid commission?

Yes, private sale is legal in New Zealand and avoids commission, but you take on pricing, marketing, buyer screening, negotiation and legal paperwork yourself, and private sellers do not have an agent's buyer database or negotiating experience. Many sellers get an agent appraisal either way to understand their price.
