Five questions to ask before choosing your agent

1. Which sales are relevant to my home?

Ask for comparable sales and an explanation of differences in condition, land, title, location and timing. A nearby sale alone does not establish your selling price.

Explore selected sold properties

2. What will you do before launch?

Ask which repairs, presentation changes and documents need attention, and where spending is unlikely to help. The preparation plan should suit your budget and likely buyers.

3. How will the marketing reach buyers?

Ask what is included, what costs extra and how photography, property portals, digital advertising and buyer follow-up will work together.

4. How will you handle feedback and offers?

Agree how you will receive updates. Ask how buyer questions, price feedback, offer conditions and negotiation will be discussed with you.

5. What are the fees and commitments?

Request a clear explanation of commission, marketing costs and the agency agreement before signing. Compare the proposed service and evidence as well as the fee.

Choosing a West Auckland Agent: Your Questions

How do I choose a real estate agent in West Auckland?

Look for an agent who understands your local suburb, explains pricing clearly, has a strong marketing plan and communicates honestly. Reviews, recent local activity and negotiation experience are also important. It is also worth checking their licence on the REA public register before you sign.

Do you offer free property appraisals in West Auckland?

Yes. Edita offers free, no-obligation written property appraisals across West Auckland. Your appraisal includes an estimated selling price range and recent comparable sales, normally delivered within one to two days of the property visit.

What suburbs do you cover?

Edita works across West Auckland and surrounding suburbs, including Blockhouse Bay, Titirangi, Lynfield, New Lynn, Glen Eden, Green Bay, Avondale, Kelston, Glendene, New Windsor and Mount Roskill.

Is a property appraisal the same as a registered valuation?

No. A real estate appraisal is an estimate of likely market value based on recent sales, current listings and buyer demand. A registered valuation is a formal valuation completed by a registered valuer.

Can you help if I am not ready to sell yet?

Yes. Many homeowners ask for advice months before selling. Early guidance can help with presentation, repairs, timing, pricing expectations and understanding the current market.
