Are New Windsor House Prices Going Up or Down in November 2026?

The suburb median moved from $1,161,000 to $988,000 (-14.9%), while the average moved from $1,174,427 to $1,020,082 (-13.1%).

Sales increased from 116 to 139 (+19.8%), while median selling time moved from 40 to 43 days.

Standard residences recorded a median of $1,111,500, compared with $1,172,500 previously.

How Has the New Windsor Property Market Changed Over the Past Year?

What Types of Homes Are Selling Best in New Windsor?

Standard residences: 94 sales, median $1,111,500 versus 90 sales at $1,172,500 previously.

Townhouses: 19 sales, median $773,000 versus 5 sales at $915,000. The current sample is much larger than last year.

Units: 21 sales, median $780,000 versus 10 sales at $1,247,500. The property mix within the unit category has changed significantly, so treat the median comparison cautiously.

Do Freehold or Cross-Lease Homes Sell Better in New Windsor?

Freehold: 115 sales, median $1,035,000 versus 102 sales at $1,167,500.

Cross-lease: 12 sales, median $925,000 versus 6 sales at $1,005,500.

There is no fixed title discount. Land, parking, shared access, alterations, condition and flats-plan accuracy all affect the result.

Which Bedroom Sizes Are Selling Most in New Windsor?

Three bedrooms: 56 sales, median $989,000.

Four bedrooms: 23 sales, median $1,085,000.

Two bedrooms: 11 sales, median $677,000. Small bedroom groups should be treated cautiously.

Is Auction or Private Treaty Working Better in New Windsor?

Auction: 37 sales, median $1,194,000, median 34 days.

Private treaty / negotiation: 102 sales, median $902,500, median 54.5 days.

These groups contain different properties and buyer pools, so the figures do not prove one method automatically produces a better result.

What Are New Windsor Buyers Looking for in 2026?

Family buyers still care about practical layout, parking, land, condition and access toward Mount Roskill, Blockhouse Bay and the motorway network.

Buyers also compare New Windsor against nearby suburbs at similar price points. See New Windsor versus Blockhouse Bay.

How Should I Price and Prepare My New Windsor Home for Sale?

  • Use recent comparable sales from the same property type and title.
  • Do not anchor to last year’s materially higher median.
  • Make parking, land and outdoor living obvious.
  • Have title, LIM and consent information ready early.
  • For cross-lease homes, check flats-plan accuracy before launch.
  • Act quickly if repeated buyer feedback points to price.

Which Local Features Matter Most to New Windsor Buyers?

  • Access toward Mount Roskill and Blockhouse Bay
  • Parking and practical family layouts
  • Usable outdoor space
  • School and everyday convenience
  • Traditional homes versus townhouse competition
  • Title, maintenance and documentation confidence

What Is the New Windsor Property Market Outlook for the Next 3–6 Months?

More sales suggest healthy buyer engagement, but the lower annual price measures show that buyers are negotiating from a different value base than a year ago.

The latest Aug–Oct quarter recorded a median of $1,134,000 from 24 sales, compared with a median of $1,172,500 from 30 sales a year earlier.
