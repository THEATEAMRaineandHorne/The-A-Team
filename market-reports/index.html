Reading The Numbers

Why suburb figures can move more than real values

Property mix

A suburb median blends houses, townhouses, units and apartments. When more of one type sells, the median moves even if values have not. See why the New Lynn median looks lower than house prices.

Outliers

One unusual sale can drag an average for a whole year. Mount Roskill is a good example: why its average price misled.

Small markets

Suburbs with fewer sales swing more from period to period. Glen Eden house prices explained shows how the sales window changes the picture.

FAQs

Market Report Questions

Are these reports valuations?

No. They are general market commentary only. A property-specific appraisal is still needed before making a pricing decision.

Why use REINZ sales?

REINZ sales exports help show actual recorded sales evidence, which is more useful than relying only on asking prices or online estimates.

How often are these reports updated?

Every month. Each report is refreshed with the latest rolling 12 months of REINZ sales and compared with the 12 months before.

Why do the median and average price differ?

The median is the middle sale, so a few very high or low sales barely move it. The average adds every sale together, so one unusual sale can pull it a long way.

Can I use a report to price my home?

Use it as context only. Your likely selling range depends on your land, condition, title, presentation and the most relevant recent sales, which an appraisal checks.
