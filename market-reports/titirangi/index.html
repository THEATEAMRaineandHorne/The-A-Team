Titirangi Property Market Report – November 2026

Titirangi’s median and average prices are firmer than a year ago and homes are selling faster, but substantially fewer properties changed hands. Buyers remain selective and property-specific differences matter enormously here.

Are Titirangi House Prices Going Up or Down in November 2026?

The overall median moved from $1,020,000 to $1,045,000 (+2.5%), while the average moved from $1,074,679 to $1,125,120 (+4.7%).

Sales fell from 277 to 203 (-26.7%), but median selling time improved from 48 to 44 days.

Standard residences recorded a median of $1,042,500 versus $1,071,500 previously. This is a much more useful comparison for a typical Titirangi house than the overall suburb median alone.

What Is Driving the Titirangi Property Market Right Now?

Titirangi remains one of the most property-specific suburbs in West Auckland. Bush, privacy, views and character can create strong emotional appeal, while sun, access, drainage, retaining and maintenance can materially affect buyer confidence.

That is why two homes with similar floor areas can attract very different buyer responses. The November figures show firmer headline prices, but much lower turnover means sellers still need a clear value story.

What Types of Homes Are Selling Best in Titirangi?

Standard residences: 190 sales at a median of $1,042,500, compared with 226 sales at $1,071,500 previously.

Townhouses: 2 sales at a median of $778,500. This is a very small sample and should not be used as a broad Titirangi trend.

Units: 2 sales at a median of $711,625. Again, the sample is too small for a strong conclusion.

Do Freehold or Cross-Lease Homes Sell Better in Titirangi?

Freehold: 172 sales, median $1,070,000, compared with 240 sales at $1,045,000.

Cross-lease: 13 sales, median $832,000, compared with 18 at $825,000.

Title matters, but access, shared areas, land, alterations, condition and the flats plan can matter just as much to an individual buyer.

Which Bedroom Sizes Are Selling Most in Titirangi?

Three bedrooms: 88 sales, median $957,000.

Four bedrooms: 61 sales, median $1,135,000.

Five bedrooms: 12 sales, median $1,450,000. Smaller groups should be treated cautiously.

Is Auction or Private Treaty Working Better in Titirangi?

Auction: 52 sales, median $1,129,000, median selling time 36 days.

Private treaty / negotiation: 151 sales, median $1,025,000, median selling time 50 days.

These are different groups of homes, so the figures do not prove one method automatically creates a better result.

What Are Titirangi Buyers Looking for in 2026?

Titirangi buyers still value privacy, native bush, character, views and a sense of retreat. But practical confidence is often what determines whether they compete strongly.

Warmth, sunlight, drainage, decks, retaining, driveway access, parking and clear property documentation all matter. See what bush and character can add to a Titirangi home.

How Should I Price and Prepare My Titirangi Home for Sale?

  • Use comparable homes with similar access, sun, land, views and condition.
  • Have LIM, title and relevant consent information ready before launch where possible.
  • Deal with obvious drainage, maintenance or presentation issues that could distract buyers.
  • Show privacy, views, outdoor living and bush setting without hiding practical limitations.
  • Make parking and driveway access clear in the marketing.
  • Respond quickly if price or condition becomes a repeated objection.

Which Local Features Matter Most to Titirangi Buyers?

  • Native bush, privacy and character
  • Sun, outlook and usable outdoor areas
  • Driveway access and off-street parking
  • Drainage, retaining and weather exposure
  • Titirangi Village and local community feel
  • Maintenance, consent records and insurance confidence

What Is the Titirangi Property Market Outlook for the Next 3–6 Months?

The annual price measures are firmer and selling time is faster, but sales volume is materially lower. That points to a selective market rather than broad-based strength.

The latest Aug–Oct quarter recorded a median of $992,000 from 35 sales, compared with 42 sales a year earlier.

Well-maintained homes with good sun, access, presentation and realistic pricing should have the clearest advantage. Properties with steep access, large maintenance requirements or unclear documentation may need more negotiation.

What Are Titirangi Homeowners Asking Before They Sell?

Price, Value And Property Type

Have Titirangi house prices increased in 2026?

The rolling median is $1,045,000, up 2.5% from $1,020,000. The figure shows direction, but Titirangi homes vary too much to apply the same percentage to every property.

What types of homes are selling most in Titirangi?

Standard residences dominate the current data with 190 sales. Townhouse and unit samples are very small and should not be used as broad guides.

Are cross-lease homes worth less than freehold homes in Titirangi?

The current medians come from very different groups of homes. Access, land, alterations, shared areas, condition and the flats plan can matter as much as the tenure label.

Selling Time And Sale Method

How long does it take to sell a house in Titirangi?

The current median is 44 days, compared with 48 days previously.

Is auction better than private treaty in Titirangi?

Auction recorded a median selling time of 36 days, while private treaty and negotiation recorded 50 days. These are different property groups, so the numbers do not prove one method is automatically superior.

Why do some Titirangi homes take much longer to sell?

Difficult access, deferred maintenance, dampness, drainage, retaining, unclear documentation or unrealistic pricing can all reduce buyer confidence.

Bush, Access And Property Condition

How much can sun, privacy or a good outlook add to a Titirangi home?

There is no reliable fixed premium in the supplied REINZ data. The strongest evidence comes from nearby comparable homes with similar access, orientation, outlook and condition.

How much are retaining, drainage and hillside issues affecting buyers?

They can materially affect confidence when work is required or information is unclear. Buyers may look closely at LIM information, drainage, retaining, insurance and natural-hazard information.

Should I fix visible maintenance before selling my Titirangi home?

Small obvious problems are often worth addressing because buyers may use them as a signal that larger maintenance has been deferred. Bigger projects should be assessed individually.

Timing And Next Steps

Is now a good time to sell in Titirangi?

Prices are firmer and selling speed has improved, but sales volume is much lower. A well-positioned home can still perform well when pricing, presentation and documentation are right.

How much could my Titirangi home be worth now?

The suburb median is only a starting point. A useful appraisal should compare your home with recent sales that match its access, sun, land, outlook, condition and maintenance profile.
