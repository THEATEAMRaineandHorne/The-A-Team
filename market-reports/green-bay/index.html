Are Green Bay House Prices Going Up or Down in November 2026?

The median moved from $1,100,000 to $1,052,500 (-4.3%). The average moved from $1,092,201 to $1,122,618 (+2.8%). The current average includes one $3,770,000 sale of four homes in a single deal. Without that sale the average was $1,083,104, down 0.8%.

Sales fell from 72 to 68 (-5.6%), while median selling time moved from 41 to 42 days.

Standard residences were much steadier at $1,100,000 versus $1,105,000.

Three bedrooms: 30 sales, median $1,042,500.

Is Auction or Private Treaty Working Better in Green Bay?

Auction: 15 sales, median $1,305,000, median 35 days.

Private treaty / negotiation: 53 sales, median $1,000,000, median 46 days.

Different homes use different methods, so this is not proof that one method is automatically better.

What Are Green Bay Buyers Looking for in 2026?

Buyers often respond to sunlight, usable land, parking, easy access, privacy and practical family layouts.

How Should I Price and Prepare My Green Bay Home for Sale?

  • Use recent comparable sales with similar land, access, title and condition.
  • Make sun, outdoor living, views and parking obvious.
  • Verify school-zone information before advertising it.
  • Have title, LIM and relevant consent information ready.
  • Act quickly on repeated buyer feedback about price.

Which Local Features Matter Most to Green Bay Buyers?

  • Sun and usable outdoor living
  • Parking and driveway access
  • Outlook, privacy and harbour-side feel
  • Traditional sections and family layouts
  • Schools, reserves and convenience
  • Maintenance and clear documentation

What Is the Green Bay Property Market Outlook for the Next 3–6 Months?

Green Bay remains selective, and its relatively small sales base means quarterly figures can move quickly.

The latest Aug–Oct quarter recorded a median of $1,109,000 from 12 sales, compared with 22 sales a year earlier.

Well-presented family homes with good sun, access and realistic pricing should have the clearest advantage.
