Opening Summary

What The REINZ Data Is Showing

Green Bay recorded more transaction activity and faster selling time, but the suburb median softened.

The cleaned REINZ comparison shows a median sale price of $1,030,000, compared with $1,107,600 in the previous 12 months. That is a decrease of 7.0%.

The average sale price moved from $1,101,136 to $1,118,809, an increase of 1.6%. The average is lifted by one grouped four-title Pendlebury Street transaction at $3.77 million, even after that transaction is counted only once.

Median days to sell improved from 42 days to 40 days. Unique transaction volume increased from 66 to 81, a rise of 22.7%.

What Is Happening In Green Bay

Green Bay Market Report

Green Bay remains a tightly held family suburb with established streets, local schools, parks and convenient access toward Blockhouse Bay, Titirangi, New Lynn and wider West Auckland.

The latest data shows a mixed result: more transactions and slightly faster selling time, but a lower median. This suggests buyers are participating, although price and property quality still matter.

For vendors, strong presentation, realistic comparable evidence, parking, sun, privacy, land usability and clear documentation can materially affect buyer confidence.

Buyer Behaviour

Standard residences represented 88.9% of cleaned latest transactions, confirming that family homes remain Green Bay’s main market.

Three-bedroom properties recorded a median of approximately $1,018,000, while four-bedroom properties recorded approximately $1,124,500.

Common buyer concerns include overpricing, dated interiors, renovation cost, dampness, parking, road position, access, land contour, cross-lease arrangements and incomplete consent information.

Property Type And Tenure

Residences recorded a median of $1,093,000. Townhouses recorded $692,000, while the single cleaned unit transaction recorded $688,000.

Freehold properties recorded a median of approximately $1,093,000, while cross-lease properties recorded approximately $908,750.

Townhouse, unit and cross-lease samples are small, so an individual property should be compared against direct local evidence rather than the category median alone.

Sale Method And Negotiation

The latest period included 21 auction transactions, with a median of $1,275,000 and median selling time of 35 days.

Private treaty and negotiation accounted for 60 transactions, with a median of $985,000 and median selling time of 47 days.

The auction sample contains more higher-value family homes, so the figures do not prove that one sale method automatically produces a better result.

Advice For Green Bay Sellers

  • Use recent comparable sales from the correct property type, location, tenure and condition bracket.
  • Prepare the LIM, title, consent information and useful maintenance records before launch.
  • Present natural light, outdoor usability, parking, privacy and practical family layout clearly.
  • Address gardens, moisture, visible maintenance, dated presentation and access concerns before photography.
  • Price to create genuine enquiry during the first weeks rather than chasing the market later.
  • Track second viewings, document requests, buyer objections and direct price feedback.

Local Factors Affecting Green Bay

  • School, park and family-lifestyle appeal
  • Access toward Blockhouse Bay, Titirangi and New Lynn
  • Sun, privacy, land contour and usable outdoor areas
  • Parking, access and road position
  • Freehold and cross-lease ownership
  • Condition, title, consent records and presentation

3–6 Month Outlook

Overall Green Bay Market Outlook

Green Bay should remain active but selective. Buyers are likely to respond to well-presented family homes while remaining careful about price, condition and future costs.

Best opportunities

Well-presented family homes, practical freehold properties, homes with good sun, parking, privacy and usable outdoor space.

Biggest risks

Overpricing, deferred maintenance, difficult access, poor presentation, land-contour concerns, cross-lease uncertainty and renovation-cost worries.

Best strategy

Use evidence-based pricing, prepare documents before launch, improve presentation and negotiate carefully around buyer conditions.

Seller Questions

Green Bay Market Report FAQs

Is Green Bay a good market for sellers in August 2026?

Transaction activity increased and selling time improved, but the median is softer. Accurate pricing and presentation remain important.

Why did the average rise while the median fell?

The latest sample contains a grouped four-title Pendlebury Street transaction at $3.77 million. It is counted once, but it still lifts the average.

Should I renovate before selling?

Not always. Focus first on work that improves buyer confidence: repairs, moisture, presentation, lighting, gardens, documentation and obvious maintenance.
