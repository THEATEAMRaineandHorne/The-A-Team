Market Update

What Is Happening In Green Bay?

The market softened on price, while selling time stayed broadly steady.

Green Bay’s median sale price was $1,030,000 over the past 12 months, compared with $1,120,000 a year earlier.

The average sale price was $1,122,692, compared with $1,106,726.

Standard residences had a median of $1,090,000, down from $1,131,000. That tells us the softer result was not only a change in property mix.

There were 78 sales, compared with 75. Median selling time was 40.5 days, almost the same as the previous 41 days.

Local Market

Green Bay Market Report

Green Bay is still mainly a traditional family-home market. Sun, flat access, usable outdoor space and proximity to parks and schools can carry real weight with buyers.

It also sits in an interesting position between Titirangi’s bush appeal and New Lynn’s higher-density convenience. That gives Green Bay a different buyer story from either neighbour.

Property Type

There were 69 residence sales, with a median of $1,090,000.

Townhouses recorded only 7 sales, while units recorded just 2. Those samples are too small for strong conclusions about the wider market.

Tenure And Title

Freehold was the largest group, with 62 sales and a median of $1,072,500.

Cross-lease properties recorded 6 sales, with a median of $908,750. That is a small sample, so it should not be treated as a fixed title discount or premium.

Bedroom Sales

Three-bedroom homes were the largest known-bedroom group, with 34 sales and a median of $1,027,500.

Four-bedroom homes recorded 19 sales at a median of $1,100,000. Two-bedroom homes had only 8 sales.

Sale Method

There were 19 auction sales, with a median of $1,305,000 and a median selling time of 35 days.

Private treaty and negotiation accounted for 59 sales, with a median of $1,000,000 and a 56-day median selling time.

That does not prove one method is better. Different homes suit different campaigns.

Advice For Green Bay Sellers

  • Sell the lifestyle properly: sun, bush feel, flat access, parks and outdoor space.
  • Do not rely only on school-zone language. Verify the current zone before advertising it.
  • If your property has a coastal outlook, show both the view and the practical site information clearly.
  • Use the smaller supply of comparable standalone homes to your advantage, but do not assume it guarantees competition.
  • Choose open-home times that make access and the property feel easy.

Green Bay Outlook: Next 3–6 Months

The annual price picture is softer, but selling time is stable and the market remains strongly house-led.

The latest June–August quarter finished at a median of $1,050,000, with a 47.5-day median selling time.

Good family homes with sun, usable sections and a strong lifestyle story should still attract buyers, but price needs to match the evidence.

For Green Bay Homeowners

Questions Homeowners Are Asking Right Now

Green Bay’s appeal is very local: school access, sun, bush feel, parks, coast and easy sections can all matter.

Lifestyle, School Zones And Coastal Appeal

How do I attract Titirangi buyers to a sunny, flatter Green Bay home?

Do not pretend Green Bay is Titirangi. Sell the real advantage: bush atmosphere where it exists, but with easier access, flatter sections and often simpler day-to-day maintenance. Buyers who like the west but want less slope or dampness may see that as a genuine upgrade in practicality.

How much value does being in the Green Bay school zones add?

The REINZ data does not give us a reliable dollar premium for school zoning. What it does tell us is that family homes dominate Green Bay. If school access matters to your buyers, verify the current enrolment zone before marketing the property and make that information easy to understand.

How should I market a Green Bay coastal or water-view property when the tide goes out?

Sell the whole setting, not just a high-tide photograph. Buyers will notice the tidal nature of the Manukau. The stronger story is outlook, privacy, birdlife, reserve access and the feel of the property. If coastal or hazard information applies, have the LIM and relevant information ready rather than avoiding the question.

Parks, Supply And Getting Buyers Through The Door

Can being close to Craigavon Park add real lifestyle value?

Yes, especially for buyers who value walking, green space and easy outdoor access. It can appeal to families and pet owners alike. I would not put a fixed dollar premium on it, but if the walk is genuinely easy and flat, it deserves to be part of the marketing story.

Does Green Bay’s smaller townhouse supply make a standalone home more valuable?

It can make a traditional family home feel harder to replace, but it does not guarantee a bidding war. The current REINZ sample is still overwhelmingly residence-based, with only 7 townhouse sales. That supports the idea that standalone-style housing remains a major part of Green Bay’s buyer market.

How should I time open homes around Portage Road and Godley Road traffic?

Choose a time when the drive into the suburb feels easy and buyers are not arriving frustrated. Weekend traffic patterns are usually different from weekday peaks, but the exact street still matters. The first visit should make Green Bay feel convenient, not complicated.
