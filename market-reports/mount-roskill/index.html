Are Mount Roskill House Prices Going Up or Down in November 2026?

The suburb median moved from $948,330 to $1,020,000 (+7.6%), while the average moved from $1,090,590 to $1,134,223 (+4.0%).

Sales fell from 434 to 363 (-16.4%), while median selling time improved from 43 to 42 days.

Standard residences recorded a median of $1,100,000, compared with $1,050,001 previously.

What Are Mount Roskill Buyers Looking for in 2026?

Buyers are comparing traditional family homes with townhouses, units and properties where land or redevelopment potential may contribute to value. Parking, usable land, layout, condition and access remain important.

For context on why the average can move differently from the median, see Mount Roskill’s average price explained.

How Should I Price and Prepare My Mount Roskill Home for Sale?

  • Use comparable sales from the same property type and similar land size.
  • Separate owner-occupier value from genuine development potential.
  • Have LIM, title and consent information ready where possible.
  • Make parking, access and usable outdoor space obvious.
  • For cross-lease homes, check flats-plan accuracy before launch.
  • Use repeated buyer feedback quickly if price becomes the main objection.

Which Local Features Matter Most to Mount Roskill Buyers?

  • Central Auckland access and transport connections
  • Traditional family homes versus newer townhouse supply
  • Land size, shape and development practicality
  • Parking and driveway access
  • School-zone and neighbourhood convenience
  • Title, consent and property-condition confidence

What Are Mount Roskill Homeowners Asking Before They Sell?

Have Mount Roskill house prices increased?

The rolling median is $1,020,000, up 7.6% from $948,330.

How long does it take to sell a house in Mount Roskill?

The current median is 42 days, compared with 43 days previously.

What types of homes sell most in Mount Roskill?

Standard residences are the largest group, followed by townhouses and units.

Are cross-lease homes worth less than freehold homes?

There is no fixed discount. Land, access, parking, shared areas, condition and flats-plan accuracy matter.

Does development potential still matter?

It can, but only where site shape, zoning, services and feasibility support it. Sellers should avoid assuming land size alone creates a development premium.
