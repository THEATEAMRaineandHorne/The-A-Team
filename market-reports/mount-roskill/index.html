Market Update

What Is Happening In Mt Roskill?

The overall numbers point to a steadier, firmer market than a year ago.

Mt Roskill’s median sale price was $980,000 over the past 12 months, compared with $940,000 a year earlier.

The average was $1,084,728, compared with $1,073,081. The median is still the better starting point because Mt Roskill has a wide range of property types and price points.

Standard residences had a median of $1,095,556, up from $1,060,000. That matters because it shows the improvement was not just a mix effect.

There were 417 sales, compared with 456. Homes took a median of 41 days to sell, down from 43 days.

Local Market

Mt Roskill Market Report

Mt Roskill buyers are often weighing convenience, land, school access, transport, layout and title. The suburb can feel very different from one pocket to another.

That is why suburb-wide figures need context. A solid brick unit, a standalone family home, a townhouse and a larger development-style site do not compete in the same buyer pool.

Property Type

There were 282 residence sales, with a median of $1,095,556. That was up from $1,060,000 a year earlier.

Townhouses recorded 65 sales at a median of $859,000. Units recorded 58 sales at a median of $852,000.

Apartments were only a small part of the current sample, with 3 sales. Small categories can shift sharply from year to year.

Tenure And Title

Freehold was the largest group, with 298 sales and a median of $1,011,500.

Cross-lease properties recorded 62 sales, with a median of $907,000. Strata properties recorded 8 sales.

Those figures are not a simple title premium. Property type, land, condition and location vary across each title structure.

Bedroom Sales

Three-bedroom homes were the largest known-bedroom group, with 150 sales and a median of $924,500.

Two-bedroom homes recorded 49 sales at a median of $730,000. Four-bedroom homes recorded 62 sales at a median of $1,141,500.

Five-bedroom and 6+ bedroom homes had smaller samples, so those medians should be treated carefully.

Sale Method

There were 75 auction sales, with a median of $1,222,000 and a median selling time of 37 days.

Private treaty and negotiation accounted for 340 sales, with a median of $925,000 and a 43-day median selling time.

That does not prove one method is always better. The right campaign depends on the property and likely buyer competition.

What Buyers Are Looking At

Buyers look closely at access, parking, road noise, school zones, land usability and how flexible the layout is for modern family living.

Some are chasing traditional family homes. Others are comparing lower-maintenance units and townhouses. In denser pockets, privacy and parking can become bigger talking points.

Local Changes To Watch

Mt Roskill continues to change, particularly where new housing intensification is reshaping parts of the suburb. That creates a mixed market: some buyers focus on convenience and new infrastructure, while others are more sensitive to density and street feel.

The suburb also benefits from strong transport links and broad city access. For some homes that is a major advantage. For others, especially near busier corridors, noise and traffic need to be handled well in the marketing.

Advice For Mt Roskill Sellers

  • Use the most relevant nearby sales. A broad suburb median is not enough on its own in a mixed suburb like Mt Roskill.
  • If the property is near a busy road, think carefully about open-home timing, sound, privacy and presentation.
  • Have LIM, title and consent information ready early, especially if the home has had alterations or sits on a cross-lease title.
  • Sell the practical advantages clearly: access, layout, parking, sun, school appeal and usable outdoor space.
  • If the home suits downsizers or first-home buyers, keep the presentation simple and easy to understand.

Mt Roskill Outlook: Next 3–6 Months

The annual numbers point to a firmer market than a year ago, even though total sales were lower. Prices improved and selling time tightened slightly.

The latest June–August quarter finished at a median of $1,022,500, with a 42.5-day median selling time.

Well-positioned homes should continue to attract attention, but buyers will still compare carefully. Clear pricing, clean presentation and good documentation remain important.

For Mt Roskill Homeowners

Questions Homeowners Are Asking Right Now

These are the kinds of questions owners are asking when they are trying to weigh up value, timing and how their street is changing.

Neighbourhood Change, School Zones And Location

How is nearby Kāinga Ora or other intensification affecting the value of my Mt Roskill home?

There is no single rule. Some buyers are cautious about density, while others focus more on access, the home itself and long-term convenience. In practice, the effect is very street-specific. The strongest defence is not a general argument. It is showing buyers the most relevant comparable sales for your type of home in your immediate area.

If I am outside a key school zone, can my home still compete strongly?

Yes, but it needs to be marketed on the right strengths. Some buyers are zone-driven, but many still prioritise land, layout, condition, transport and value. A home just outside a key zone may still attract strong interest if it solves the family’s space and budget problem better than the in-zone alternatives.

How much does sun, elevation or a better position around Puketāpapa matter?

Quite a lot. Good sun, an open outlook and a more usable site can lift buyer response. The reverse is also true. A home that feels dark, shaded or awkwardly exposed can be harder to sell. In Mt Roskill, these small position differences can matter more than owners expect.

Noise, Lifestyle Appeal And Older Property Types

Does being near SH20 help because of access, or hurt because of noise?

It can do both. Strong motorway access is a genuine selling point, especially for buyers who commute across the city or to the airport. But if traffic noise is noticeable, the presentation needs to be handled carefully. Open-home timing, window treatment, privacy and the way the home feels indoors all become more important.

Are agents missing the lifestyle angle of the Dominion Road side of Mt Roskill?

Sometimes. For the right buyer, the food scene, transport access and city connection are all part of the appeal. Not every Mt Roskill home should be marketed the same way. Some suit families first. Others suit buyers who want a lower-maintenance base close to everything.

How do I position an older brick-and-tile unit against newer townhouses?

Play to the strengths buyers already care about: solid construction, easier layout, lower maintenance surprises, practical storage and straightforward living. In this report, units recorded a median of $852,000 from 58 sales, so they remain an active part of the market. The buyer pool is there if the price and presentation feel sensible.
