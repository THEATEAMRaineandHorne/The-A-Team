Opening Summary

What The REINZ Data Is Showing

The median improved and selling time became slightly faster, while cleaned transaction volume was modestly lower.

The cleaned median moved from $960,000 to $980,000, an increase of 2.1%.

Cleaned transaction volume moved from 395 to 376, a decrease of 4.8%. Median days to sell improved from 43 to 41 days.

The average fell from $1,108,001 to $1,052,793. The previous average was lifted by a $15.73 million residential section transaction, so the median and direct comparable sales are more useful for most homeowners.

Local Market

Mount Roskill Market Report

Mount Roskill is a broad, well-connected Auckland market containing apartments, units, townhouses, cross-lease homes, family residences and development-influenced sites.

The latest median improved, but not every property type moved equally. Buyers continue comparing Mount Roskill against Three Kings, Lynfield, Hillsborough, Sandringham, Blockhouse Bay and New Windsor.

For sellers, direct comparable sales matter more than a single suburb average.

Buyer Behaviour

The latest cleaned period recorded 275 residences, 69 townhouses, 22 units and 3 apartments.

Three-bedroom properties recorded a median of $920,000, four-bedroom properties recorded $1,148,000, and five-bedroom properties recorded $1,344,000.

Buyers remain careful about condition, parking, access, consent history, renovation cost, cross-lease arrangements and body-corporate obligations.

Property Type And Tenure

Residences recorded a median of $1,075,000. Townhouses recorded $860,000, units recorded $867,000, and apartments recorded $450,000.

Freehold properties recorded a median of $1,030,000, while cross-lease properties recorded $933,500.

Small property categories should be interpreted carefully and compared with directly similar homes.

Sale Method And Negotiation

The latest period included 79 auction transactions, with a median of $1,225,000 and median selling time of 38 days.

Private treaty and negotiation accounted for 295 transactions, with a median of $930,000 and median selling time of 44.5 days.

Different methods contain different property mixes, so the figures do not prove that one method automatically gives a better result.

Advice For Mount Roskill Sellers

  • Use recent comparable sales from the correct property type and tenure.
  • Prepare the LIM, title, consent records and renovation information before launch.
  • Address moisture, gardens, lighting, paint and visible maintenance before photography.
  • Explain parking, access, body-corporate and cross-lease details clearly.
  • Track repeat viewings, document requests and direct buyer objections.
  • Negotiate carefully around finance, building reports and settlement timing.

Local Factors Affecting Mount Roskill

  • Central-west Auckland and motorway access
  • Comparison with Three Kings, Lynfield and Hillsborough
  • Freehold, cross-lease and strata ownership
  • Parking, access, sun and usable land
  • Development potential and unusual section sales
  • Condition, documentation and renovation cost

3–6 Month Outlook

Mount Roskill Market Outlook

The market should remain active but selective, with buyers rewarding well-presented homes and clear value.

Best opportunities

Well-presented family homes, practical freehold properties and homes with good parking, sun and clear records.

Biggest risks

Overpricing, deferred maintenance, unclear consent history, difficult access and using development sales as ordinary comparables.

Best strategy

Use direct evidence, prepare documentation early and respond quickly to buyer feedback.

Seller Questions

Mount Roskill Market Report FAQs

Is Mount Roskill a good market for sellers?

The median improved and selling time became faster, but buyers remain selective. Pricing and presentation still matter.

Why did the average fall?

The previous period includes a very high-value residential section transaction. The median is a safer central measure.

Should I renovate before selling?

Not always. Focus first on repairs, moisture, presentation, lighting, gardens and clear documentation.
