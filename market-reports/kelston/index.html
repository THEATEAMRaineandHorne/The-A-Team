Are Kelston House Prices Going Up or Down in November 2026?

The median moved from $782,000 to $800,000 (+2.3%), while the average moved from $849,643 to $812,146 (-4.4%). The previous-year average includes one $3,265,000 sale of six units in a single deal. Without that sale the average was $815,624, so the fall was closer to 0.4%.

Sales fell from 72 to 63 (-12.5%), and median selling time increased from 45 to 63.5 days.

Standard residences recorded a median of $830,000 compared with $802,500 previously.

What Types of Homes Are Selling Best in Kelston?

Standard residences: 46 sales, median $830,000 versus 52 sales at $802,500 previously.

Townhouses: 16 sales, median $679,000 versus 12 sales at $620,000.

Units: No unit sales in the current period, compared with 3 at $555,000 previously. One $2,150,000 whole-site sale recorded as a unit is not counted here.

Do Freehold or Cross-Lease Homes Sell Better in Kelston?

Freehold: 57 sales, median $802,000 versus 62 sales at $797,500.

Cross-lease: 3 sales, median $685,000. This is a very small sample, so it should not be used as a fixed title discount.

Is Auction or Private Treaty Working Better in Kelston?

Auction: 5 sales, median $860,000, median 57 days.

Private treaty / negotiation: 58 sales, median $790,250, median 65 days.

These groups contain different homes, so the figures do not prove one method is automatically better.

What Are Kelston Buyers Looking for in 2026?

Buyers are comparing standalone homes with townhouse alternatives. Land, parking, storage, fencing, usable outdoor space and straightforward layouts can create a clear advantage.

Older homes can still appeal strongly when maintenance feels manageable and title, LIM and consent information are easy to understand.

How Should I Price and Prepare My Kelston Home for Sale?

  • Price against recent Kelston comparables, not only nearby New Lynn.
  • Show land, garaging, parking and backyard space clearly.
  • Check title, LIM and consent information before launch.
  • Time open homes to minimise traffic and parking frustration.
  • Have drainage or overland-flow information ready where relevant.
  • Act on repeated buyer feedback quickly.

Which Local Features Matter Most to Kelston Buyers?

  • Standalone-home value versus townhouse supply
  • Backyard, fencing and off-street parking
  • Access toward New Lynn and West Auckland
  • School-cluster convenience and traffic patterns
  • Title, consent and property-condition confidence
  • Flood, drainage and overland-flow information where relevant

What Is the Kelston Property Market Outlook for the Next 3–6 Months?

The median is slightly firmer, but the lower average, lower turnover and longer selling time show that buyers remain cautious.

The latest Aug–Oct quarter recorded a median of $850,000 from 8 sales, compared with 23 sales a year earlier.

Realistic pricing and a strong first impression remain the biggest seller advantages.
