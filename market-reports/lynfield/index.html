Are Lynfield House Prices Going Up or Down in November 2026?

The median moved from $1,175,000 to $1,040,000 (-11.5%), while the average moved from $1,215,779 to $1,107,836 (-8.9%).

Sales increased from 61 to 67 (+9.8%), while median selling time improved from 43 to 39.5 days.

Standard residences recorded a median of $1,094,500, compared with $1,225,000 previously.

What Types of Homes Are Selling Best in Lynfield?

Standard residences: 58 sales, median $1,094,500 versus 47 sales at $1,225,000 previously.

Townhouses: 3 sales, median $950,000. This is a small sample.

Units: 4 sales, median $642,500. This sample is also small, so treat it cautiously.

Do Freehold or Cross-Lease Homes Sell Better in Lynfield?

Freehold: 51 sales, median $1,075,000 versus 47 sales at $1,225,000.

Cross-lease: 6 sales, median $804,000 versus 11 sales at $900,000.

Cross-lease results are based on small samples, so land, access, parking, condition and flats-plan accuracy matter more than a simple title discount.

Which Bedroom Sizes Are Selling Most in Lynfield?

Three bedrooms: 12 sales, median $1,005,000.

Four bedrooms: 15 sales, median $1,100,000.

Five bedrooms: 4 sales, median $1,185,000. Smaller bedroom groups are more volatile.

Is Auction or Private Treaty Working Better in Lynfield?

Auction: 14 sales, median $1,259,500, median 37 days.

Private treaty / negotiation: 53 sales, median $1,040,000, median 46 days.

These are different groups of properties, so the figures do not prove one method automatically produces a better result.

What Are Lynfield Buyers Looking for in 2026?

Family buyers often focus on practical layout, garaging or off-street parking, sun, usable outdoor space and confidence around maintenance.

School-zone appeal remains important for some buyers. See how the Lynfield College zone can influence property values.

How Should I Price and Prepare My Lynfield Home for Sale?

  • Use recent Lynfield sales from the same property type and similar land size.
  • Do not anchor to older peak sales if current evidence is lower.
  • Make garaging, parking, sun and outdoor living obvious.
  • Have title, LIM and relevant consent information ready early.
  • For cross-lease homes, check flats-plan accuracy before launch.
  • Respond quickly if several buyers repeat the same price concern.

Which Local Features Matter Most to Lynfield Buyers?

  • School-zone appeal
  • Family-friendly layouts
  • Parking and garaging
  • Sun and usable outdoor areas
  • Access toward Mount Roskill and the motorway network
  • Condition and documentation confidence
