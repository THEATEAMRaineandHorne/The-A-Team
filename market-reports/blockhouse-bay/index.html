Direct Answer

What Is The Blockhouse Bay Property Market Doing?

The September data points to firmer headline pricing, stable annual selling time and lower transaction volume, with property mix playing a meaningful role in the price comparison.

The cleaned suburb median increased from $1,042,500 to $1,100,000, a rise of 5.5%. The average increased 5.4% to $1,140,469.

For residences—the dominant category—the median moved from $1,100,000 to $1,123,000, an increase of 2.1%. This is a more useful reference for many homeowners than the all-property median because the previous period contained 40 unit sales and 23 townhouse sales, compared with only 11 units and 8 townhouses in the latest period.

Median days to sell was 41 days in both periods. Cleaned transaction volume fell from 292 to 261, a decline of 10.6%. Residence sales, however, increased from 223 to 236, showing that the overall volume decline was heavily influenced by fewer lower-density and development-style transactions.

Local Interpretation

Blockhouse Bay Market Report

Blockhouse Bay remains an established West Auckland family suburb, with buyers attracted by its village centre, schools, parks, coastal and reserve access, and convenient links toward New Lynn, Lynfield, Green Bay and Mount Roskill.

The September data suggests values for standard residences have been reasonably resilient, but the broader market is still selective. Sellers should price against truly comparable homes rather than using the stronger all-property median as a blanket guide.

Properties with practical layout, useful parking, good sun, tidy presentation and clear title or consent information are generally easier for buyers to assess with confidence.

Property Type And Tenure

Residences recorded 236 sales at a median of $1,123,000, compared with 223 sales at $1,100,000 previously.

Units recorded 11 sales at a median of $800,000, while townhouses recorded only 8 sales at $886,000. These smaller categories should be interpreted cautiously and property-by-property.

Freehold properties recorded 198 sales at a median of $1,135,500. Cross-lease properties recorded 30 sales at a median of $873,250.

Bedroom Analysis

Three-bedroom properties recorded 86 sales with a median of $1,058,000. Four-bedroom properties recorded 69 sales with a median of $1,144,000.

Five-bedroom properties recorded 23 sales at a median of $1,360,000. Two-bedroom properties recorded 22 sales at $793,000.

Bedroom count is only one factor. Land, renovation quality, garaging, school appeal, road position, cross-lease arrangements and indoor-outdoor flow can materially change a Blockhouse Bay sale result.

Buyer Behaviour

Three- and four-bedroom homes continue to make up the centre of the local buyer market. Buyers tend to compare Blockhouse Bay closely with Lynfield, Green Bay, New Windsor and parts of New Lynn, so perceived value against nearby alternatives matters.

Well-presented homes with good natural light, practical parking, useful outdoor space and straightforward documentation are easier to commit to. Buyers can be more cautious where significant renovation, shared-access, title or consent issues create uncertainty.

Sale Method And Negotiation

The latest period included 64 auction sales, with a median price of $1,252,500 and median selling time of 34.5 days.

Private treaty and negotiation accounted for 196 sales, with a median of $1,086,250 and median selling time of 52 days.

This does not mean one method automatically produces a higher price. Auction properties may differ in quality, value range and buyer competition. Method selection should be based on the individual home and likely buyer pool.

Advice For Blockhouse Bay Sellers

  • Base pricing on recent directly comparable Blockhouse Bay sales, not the suburb median alone.
  • Prepare title, LIM, consent and cross-lease information before launch where relevant.
  • Highlight sun, parking, family layout, storage and outdoor usability.
  • Address obvious maintenance and presentation issues before photography where practical.
  • Compare your home against nearby alternatives in Lynfield, Green Bay, New Windsor and New Lynn.
  • Track second inspections, document requests and buyer objections—not only enquiry counts.

Local Factors Affecting Blockhouse Bay

  • Blockhouse Bay Village and established community feel
  • Schools, reserves, coastal walks and family appeal
  • Access toward New Lynn, Lynfield and central Auckland
  • Freehold and cross-lease ownership differences
  • Parking, road position, sun and outdoor usability
  • Renovation quality, consent history and maintenance

Blockhouse Bay 3–6 Month Market Outlook

The latest evidence supports a steady to modestly firmer outlook for well-positioned residences rather than assuming broad 5%+ growth across every property type. Residence medians are up around 2.1% year-on-year, while annual selling time is unchanged.

Buyer selectivity is likely to remain important. Homes that launch with realistic evidence-based pricing, strong presentation and clear documentation should be better placed to create competition, while over-priced or compromised properties may take longer.

For homeowners considering a sale, the useful question is where the individual property sits within today’s competing stock—not simply whether the suburb-wide median has risen.

Seller Questions

Blockhouse Bay Market Report FAQs

Are Blockhouse Bay house prices rising in September 2026?

The cleaned all-property median is up 5.5%, but the residence-only median is up a more moderate 2.1%. The difference is important because the mix of units, townhouses and residences changed significantly.

How long are Blockhouse Bay homes taking to sell?

The cleaned annual median is 41 days, unchanged from the previous 12 months. Individual campaigns vary according to pricing, condition, location, title, presentation and buyer competition.

Why did the suburb median rise more than the residence median?

The previous period contained many more unit and townhouse transactions, which generally sold below the residence median. With fewer of those lower-priced categories in the latest mix, the headline median moved higher.

What should Blockhouse Bay sellers focus on before listing?

Use genuinely comparable sales, prepare property documents early, present the home strongly and make pricing competitive against the homes buyers will see at the same time.
