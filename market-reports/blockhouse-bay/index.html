Market Update

What Is Happening In Blockhouse Bay?

Prices are a little stronger than a year ago. Selling time is unchanged, and fewer properties changed hands.

Blockhouse Bay’s median sale price was $1.10 million over the past 12 months. A year earlier it was $1,042,500. The average sale price was $1,140,469.

For standard residences, the median was $1,123,000. A year earlier it was $1,100,000. That 2.1% increase is a better guide for many local homeowners.

Homes took a median of 41 days to sell, the same as last year. There were 261 sales, down from 292.

Local Market

Blockhouse Bay Market Report

Blockhouse Bay continues to appeal to families. The village, schools, parks and easy access to nearby suburbs all help.

Prices are a little stronger than a year ago, but buyers are still careful. A good suburb does not make an over-priced home sell.

Homes with a practical layout, good sun, useful parking and tidy presentation are easier for buyers to say yes to.

Property Type And Tenure

Most sales were standard residences. There were 236, with a median of $1,123,000. A year earlier the residence median was $1,100,000.

Only 11 units and 8 townhouses sold. Those are small samples, so their medians should not be used as a broad market guide.

There were 198 freehold sales and 30 cross-lease sales. The freehold median was $1,135,500.

Bedroom Sales

Three-bedroom homes had a median of $1,058,000. Four-bedroom homes were higher at $1,144,000.

Five-bedroom homes had a median of $1,360,000. The two-bedroom median was $793,000.

Bedroom count is only part of the picture. Land, condition, parking and the quality of the renovation can make a big difference.

What Buyers Are Looking At

Three- and four-bedroom homes make up a large part of the local market. Buyers often compare Blockhouse Bay with Lynfield, Green Bay, New Windsor and New Lynn.

Natural light, parking, outdoor space and a sensible layout matter. Buyers also notice when a home needs major work or the title and consent history are unclear.

Sale Method

There were 64 auction sales. Their median price was $1,252,500 and the median selling time was 34.5 days.

Another 196 properties sold by private treaty or negotiation. Their median was $1,086,250, with a median selling time of 52 days.

That does not make auction the right choice for every property. The method should suit the home and the buyers we expect to attract.

Advice For Blockhouse Bay Sellers

  • Use recent comparable sales. Do not rely on the suburb median alone.
  • Have the title, LIM and consent information ready early.
  • Make the most of sun, parking, storage and outdoor space.
  • Fix obvious presentation issues before the photos where practical.
  • Watch the homes competing with yours in nearby suburbs.
  • Listen to buyer feedback and respond quickly if the market is telling you something.

Local Factors Affecting Blockhouse Bay

  • Blockhouse Bay Village and established community feel
  • Schools, reserves, coastal walks and family appeal
  • Access toward New Lynn, Lynfield and central Auckland
  • Freehold and cross-lease ownership differences
  • Parking, road position, sun and outdoor usability
  • Renovation quality, consent history and maintenance

What We Expect Over The Next Few Months

I expect Blockhouse Bay to remain fairly steady. Standard residence prices are a little higher than a year ago, but buyers still have choices.

Good family homes should continue to attract interest. Over-priced homes may take longer, especially if they also need work.

For a seller, the most useful guide will be the homes competing with yours when you come to market.

Questions Homeowners Are Asking Right Now

These are the questions that come up when owners are weighing up value, timing and what to do next.

Suburb Equity And Value Realities

Which value tier does my home sit in if it has elevation or harbour views?

Three-bedroom homes had a median of $1,058,000 over the past year, but that is only a starting point. Harbour outlook, elevation, sun, land, condition and street position can move a home well above the suburb average.

How much does a cross-lease title affect value, and should I convert before selling?

Freehold made up about 75.9% of the sales in this report. Cross-lease homes can still sell very well. The impact depends on the property, the flats plan and any alterations. Converting title can be expensive, so it should be assessed before spending money on it.

Are Blockhouse Bay buyers paying close to CV, or discounting heavily?

Among sales with a valuation figure recorded, just over half sold above that figure. Around 55% sold above it, 44% below it and about 2% at it. CV is a reference point. Buyers still judge the actual home, condition, parking and position.

Market Velocity And Buyer Competition

If the median selling time is 41 days, what makes some homes sit much longer?

Price is usually the first thing to look at. Presentation, maintenance, title issues and lack of parking can also slow buyers down. A home that feels easy to understand is easier to buy.

How is the new townhouse supply in New Lynn affecting older Blockhouse Bay homes?

Buyers do compare the two areas, especially at similar price points. New townhouses can appeal to people who want low maintenance. Older Blockhouse Bay homes can still win on land, privacy, parking and family space.

Does being close to Blockhouse Bay Village add a premium?

Walkability can help, especially for buyers who value shops, schools and everyday convenience. There is no fixed percentage. The premium depends on the street, traffic, land, parking and the quality of the home.

School Zones And Property Due Diligence

How much more is a home worth if it is in the preferred local school zones?

School zones can make a home more attractive to family buyers, but there is no reliable suburb-wide dollar premium in the REINZ data we have. The best comparison is between similar nearby homes where the main difference is zoning.

How do coastal erosion or clifftop stability rules affect a harbour-edge property?

It depends on the individual site. Buyers may look closely at LIM information, hazard overlays, retaining, drainage and insurance. A harbour position can be a major positive, but any known risk needs to be understood and disclosed properly.

Will an unpermitted extension or missing CCC stop my sale?

Not always, but it can reduce the buyer pool and make finance or insurance harder. The earlier the issue is identified, the more options you have. Good documentation can make a big difference.

The Safety Net And Next Steps

If I sell now, how far could my equity go in Green Bay or Titirangi?

That depends on your likely net sale proceeds and what type of home you want next. Green Bay and Titirangi can offer very different choices in land, outlook, access and maintenance. Compare actual homes, not just suburb medians.

Could I keep my Blockhouse Bay home and rent it out instead?

Possibly. Well-presented three-bedroom homes can achieve strong rents, but the result depends on condition, parking, location and the time of year. Get a current rental appraisal before deciding to hold rather than sell.
