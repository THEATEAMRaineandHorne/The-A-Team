Blockhouse Bay Property Market Report – November 2026

Price Direction

Are Blockhouse Bay House Prices Going Up or Down in November 2026?

Both the median and average are higher than the previous 12-month period, but turnover is lower.

The median rose from $1,045,000 to $1,140,000, a change of +9.1%. The average rose from $1,078,992 to $1,175,770, a change of +9.0%.

Sales fell from 256 to 222 (-13.3%), while median selling time was 42 days versus 41 days.

Standard residences support the stronger price picture: their median increased from $1,085,000 to $1,150,000, while residence sales were steady at 201 versus 199.

Local Market

What Is Driving the Blockhouse Bay Property Market Right Now?

Blockhouse Bay remains a family-home market, with standard residences accounting for most recorded sales. Buyers continue to compare local homes with Lynfield, Green Bay, New Windsor and New Lynn, so value is judged across a wider West Auckland search area.

The stronger annual median is encouraging, but the lower number of transactions means sellers should not assume every home will attract the same depth of competition. Street position, title, condition, parking and usable outdoor space can still make a large difference.

What Types of Homes Are Selling Best in Blockhouse Bay?

Standard residences: 201 sales at a median of $1,150,000, compared with 199 sales at $1,085,000.

Townhouses: 8 sales at a median of $886,000. The sample is small, so the median should be treated cautiously.

Units: 9 sales at a median of $636,026, compared with 30 sales previously. Lower turnover makes this category more sensitive to individual transactions.

Do Freehold or Cross-Lease Homes Sell Better in Blockhouse Bay?

Freehold: 181 sales at a median of $1,160,000, up +6.1% from $1,093,500.

Cross-lease: 25 sales at a median of $905,000, compared with $880,000 previously.

These are different property groups, so the gap should not be treated as a fixed title discount. Land, shared access, alterations, flats-plan accuracy, parking and condition all matter.

Which Bedroom Sizes Are Selling Most in Blockhouse Bay?

Three bedrooms: 78 sales, median $1,075,500, median selling time 36 days.

Four bedrooms: 68 sales, median $1,157,500, median selling time 48 days.

Five bedrooms: 27 sales, median $1,270,000. This segment is smaller, so individual larger-home sales can move its median quickly.

Is Auction or Private Treaty Working Better in Blockhouse Bay?

Auction: 64 sales, median $1,281,000, median selling time 37 days.

Private treaty / negotiation: 157 sales, median $1,090,000, median selling time 53 days.

This is not evidence that auction automatically produces a better result. Different homes, price brackets and seller circumstances use different sale methods.

What Are Blockhouse Bay Buyers Looking for in 2026?

Three- and four-bedroom homes remain the biggest known-bedroom groups, which is consistent with Blockhouse Bay’s family-home character. Buyers tend to notice sunlight, practical layouts, parking, storage and usable outdoor space.

They are also alert to maintenance, consent history and title complexity. A home that is easy to understand, finance and insure can have an advantage over a competing property that creates uncertainty.

How Should I Price and Prepare My Blockhouse Bay Home for Sale?

  • Use the closest recent comparable sales, especially the same property type, title and street position.
  • Have the title, LIM and relevant consent information ready before buyers start asking.
  • Make the most of sun, parking, storage and outdoor living in the photography and open homes.
  • Fix obvious maintenance and presentation issues where the likely buyer benefit justifies the cost.
  • Watch competing homes in Lynfield, Green Bay, New Windsor and New Lynn as well as Blockhouse Bay. See how buyers compare New Windsor and Blockhouse Bay.
  • Take early buyer feedback seriously. Strong annual price figures do not rescue a campaign that starts above buyer expectations.

Which Local Features Matter Most to Blockhouse Bay Buyers?

  • Blockhouse Bay Village and established community feel
  • Schools, reserves and family-friendly amenities
  • Coastal walks and access to green space
  • Parking, road position and outdoor usability
  • Freehold and cross-lease title differences
  • Renovation quality, maintenance and documentation

What Is the Blockhouse Bay Property Market Outlook for the Next 3–6 Months?

The rolling 12-month median and average are both higher, and the residence-only median is also firmer. That gives well-positioned family homes a reasonable base going into summer.

However, annual sales volume is down 13.3%, and the latest Aug–Oct quarter recorded 40 sales compared with 57 a year earlier. That argues for realistic pricing rather than assuming the annual increase will carry every property.

The likely pattern is selective rather than uniformly strong: good homes can attract competition, while properties with condition, title or pricing issues may need longer and more negotiation.

For Blockhouse Bay Homeowners

What Are Blockhouse Bay Homeowners Asking Before They Sell?

These questions focus on value, title, timing, buyer demand and practical preparation.

Price, Value And Property Type

Have Blockhouse Bay house prices increased in 2026?

Across the current rolling 12 months, the suburb median is $1,140,000, up 9.1% from $1,045,000. Standard residences also increased from $1,085,000 to $1,150,000. That does not mean every home increased by the same percentage.

Are cross-lease properties worth less than freehold homes in Blockhouse Bay?

The current freehold median is $1,160,000 and the cross-lease median is $905,000, but they are different groups of properties. Land, house size, alterations, shared access, parking and the flats plan also matter.

What types of homes are selling most in Blockhouse Bay?

Standard residences dominate the data, with 201 sales. Townhouses and units are much smaller segments, so their medians are less reliable as a guide for the suburb as a whole.

Selling Time And Sale Method

How long does it take to sell a house in Blockhouse Bay?

The current 12-month median is 42 days, compared with 41 days previously. Individual campaigns can be much faster or slower depending on price, condition, demand and the sale method.

Is auction better than private treaty for selling in Blockhouse Bay?

Auction sales recorded a median selling time of 37 days, while private treaty and negotiation recorded 53 days. The groups contain different homes, so this is not proof that one method automatically produces the better result.

Why do some Blockhouse Bay homes sit on the market longer?

Price is usually the first issue to test. Condition, presentation, parking, title complexity, maintenance and incomplete property information can also slow buyer decisions.

Local Appeal And Due Diligence

Does being close to Blockhouse Bay Village add value?

Walkability to shops, schools and local amenities can strengthen buyer appeal, but there is no fixed percentage premium in the supplied REINZ data. Street position, traffic, parking, land and the home itself still matter.

How much do school zones affect a Blockhouse Bay property?

School zones can matter to family buyers, but the supplied REINZ data does not isolate a reliable dollar premium for zoning. Compare genuinely similar nearby homes and check current zone information separately.

Can missing consents or cross-lease alterations affect a sale?

Yes. Unclear consent history or alterations that do not match a flats plan can create questions for buyers, lenders and insurers. Finding documentation issues early gives you more options before launch.

Timing And Next Steps

Is now a good time to sell a house in Blockhouse Bay?

The rolling-year price measures are stronger, but total sales volume is lower. If your home is well presented and can be positioned realistically against current competition, the market can still provide a good selling opportunity.

How much could my Blockhouse Bay home be worth now?

The suburb median is only a starting point. A useful appraisal should compare your home with recent sales that match its street, land, title, size, condition, parking and buyer appeal.

Where Does This Blockhouse Bay Property Market Data Come From?

Source: REINZ sales data supplied for Blockhouse Bay on 4 November 2026.

Some records do not contain every field, so bedroom, tenure and days-to-sell breakdowns use the information available for those sales. Small category samples are noted and should be treated cautiously.
